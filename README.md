# 📊 Customer Insight Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes a large-scale customer dataset to uncover insights related to customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and business performance. The analysis is performed using SQL for data analysis, while the results are presented through a structured report and dashboard to support business decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives
1. Analyze customer demographics and behavior patterns
2. Identify key drivers of purchasing activity
3. Evaluate regional and product performance
4. Assess promotion and loyalty program effectiveness
5. Provide actionable insights for business strategy

## 📂 Dataset
The dataset contains 100,000 customer records, including:
1. Demographics (age, income, region, education)
2. Purchase behavior and frequency
3. Product category spending
4. Promotion usage
5. Customer loyalty tiers and satisfaction scores

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
1. SQL for data analysis
2. Tableau for dashboard creation
3. Power Point Presentation for reporting

## 📈 Key Insights
1. Customer distribution is highly concentrated in the 25–35 age range, representing the core customer segment.
2. Purchase frequency is skewed toward low engagement, with 50% of customers classified as rare buyers, indicating a major opportunity for re-engagement strategies.
3. Income and purchasing behavior show stronger influence on spending compared to education level, which has minimal impact.
4. Regional performance is dominated by East and West regions, which together contribute nearly 60% of total customers and revenue.
5. Electronics is the top-performing product category, contributing the largest share of total revenue.
6. Promotion usage is relatively low, with nearly 70% of customers not using promo codes, suggesting untapped marketing potential.
7. Customer satisfaction is consistently low across all loyalty tiers, indicating that the current loyalty program is not effectively improving customer experience.
8. High-income but low-frequency customers represent a valuable segment at risk of churn, requiring targeted retention strategies.
